Fixed

`yokoten check` no longer fails a new repo on things that are public on purpose (#1169). An email address in SECURITY.md, or in the people fields of package.json, composer.json, Cargo.toml or pyproject.toml, is now a note. Loopback and the three private ranges (RFC 1918) pass, in the commit check and in what an agent posts. The folder cap of 7 entries fails only a repo whose yokoten.json has a "layout" key. yokoten's own has one, and `yokoten init` writes one. Any other repo gets one note that lists its wide folders. A personal email anywhere else, and a public address, still fail.
