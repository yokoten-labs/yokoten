Added

`yokoten desk <issue>` gives one task to one AI in its own fenced desk. It makes the desk `../<repo>-desks/<n>/`, its own clone on the branch `desk/<n>`, writes a brief from the issue (or from a task in quotes), and starts Claude Code (or Codex, with `--agent codex`) inside the sandbox for you to watch. When the AI exits, the desk runs the commit check and the project's tests inside the sandbox and shows the evidence on one screen: clean, work not pushed, or failed. It never pushes; `yokoten desk <n> --pr` pushes the branch from your checkout and opens the pull request, as the bot when it is set up. A clean desk is saved as a git bundle and moved to `../<repo>-desks/trash/`. Bare `yokoten desk` lists your desks and writes nothing. A desk that is quiet for 20 minutes while its AI runs is marked stalled, and its work is saved in a commit. A desk that stopped mid-task shows on the next start, and `yokoten desk <n>` goes on from its last commit and its brief. On native Windows, the desk says to use WSL and starts nothing (#1054, #1056).
